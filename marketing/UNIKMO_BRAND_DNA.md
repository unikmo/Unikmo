# UNIKMO Marketing DNA — Less stuff. More us.
Status: Founder-approved positioning; campaign profile proposed for testing.
Date: 2026-11-20. Autonomy: L2 (draft for approval; no publishing or spend).

## Canonical message — DO NOT REPLACE
**Slogan / hero:** Less stuff. More us.
**Supporting line:** The gift isn't the card. It's what they feel when they open it.
**Emotional product line:** Your voice. Your words. Their private moment.
**Primary CTA:** Give them a moment.

Do not replace this with generic slogans about moments, memories, keepsakes or QR codes. Those can appear as supporting explanations, never as the top-line brand identity. "Less stuff" rejects impersonal, forgettable gifts, NOT the tangible value of UNIKMO's premium physical card. The card is an important keepsake and delivery vessel; the personal relationship is the gift.

## Brand strategy
- Emotional territory: more us, less generic stuff; clever observations about gifting, relationships and private in-jokes.
- Audience goal: feel recognized, comment voluntarily, tag a friend, share, consider giving a moment.
- Voice: intimate, witty, warm, premium, specific; never sanctimonious, saccharine, manipulative or generic inspirational quotes.
- Brand Mischief Profile (working creative ratings): humor 7/10, sarcasm 3/10, provocation 4/10, emotional intelligence 10/10, authenticity 10/10, sentimentality 6/10, surprise 10/10, selling pressure 2/10.
- People first; product is a discreet enabling object, not the hero of every social post.
- Keep UNIKMO distinct from Keys Happen: no copied joke structure or cross-brand voice.
- Privacy by design: no publishing private recipient moments, videos, or messages without clear consent. No fake testimonials, staged reactions presented as real, or manipulative death/grief hooks.
- Never promise limitless permanence, unsupported sustainability benefits, or anything not tested. Verify prices, Stripe checkout, shipping and fulfillment before sales-led creative.
- No edits to website or production deployment under this marketing PR.

## Three creative territories
1. **Less stuff** — humorous commentary about generic gifts and the annual ritual of buying things nobody wanted. Joke about the situation, not the recipient.
2. **More us** — real shared memories, affection, friendship and private jokes people wish they had recorded.
3. **The reveal** — genuine, consent-based reactions to receiving a personal message via the physical card.

## Five hooks to test
Experiment ID: UNI-2026-001. These are original draft variations for approval, not published or performance-validated.
- **A / Generic gift:** "Another mug? We can do better." Visual: tasteful shelf of repetitive generic mugs vs single elegant premium keepsake card, cream/black/gold. Comment prompt: "What's the most forgettable gift you've received? Be kind."
- **B / More us:** "The best gift is an inside joke nobody else understands." Visual: two friends laughing on sofa without legible phone content; natural candid moment. Prompt: "Tag the person who knows exactly which joke."
- **C / The unsaid words:** "What would you say if you had 30 seconds they would actually keep?" Visual: adult recording a private heartfelt voice message at a quiet kitchen table; no visible or audible personal content. Prompt: "You don't have to tell us. Just think of the person."
- **D / The reveal:** "The gift isn't the card. It's what they feel when they open it." Visual: brand-owned physical card in hands, recipient smiling at unseen content, with consent; no fabricated authentic reactions. Prompt: "Who would you give a moment to?"
- **E / Occasions:** "A birthday gift they can hear in your voice." Visual: premium physical UNIKMO card beside discreet birthday wrapping, private audio implied not exposed. Prompt: "Whose voice makes any birthday better?"

## Visual system and asset route
- Source first: existing /story, /occasions and product assets, homepage imagery, Canva and owned videos; audit permissions and visual accuracy.
- Style: premium minimalist, restrained editorial cream/black/gold, human faces and gestures, warm natural lighting, unobtrusive card; do not cheaply overload with hearts, emojis, visual clichés or heavy text.
- Route: existing assets -> crop/edit/caption -> Canva -> lightweight generation only if no owned asset fits. No premium generation without evidence and approval.
- Format: 9:16 social stories/reels plus cropped 4:5 feed variants; preserve safe negative space for captions and accessibility.
- Do not depict a generic black/gold card as the actual branded product unless the actual source asset matches.

## Editorial mix — starting hypothesis, not validated
45% relatable relationship humor/stories; 30% real emotional moments/reactions; 15% gifting occasions; 10% product explanation/offer. Do not push obligatory daily product links.

## Growth loop and test
Funny/relatable gifting insight -> genuine comments & tags -> trusted visit to product experience -> private gift creation -> recipient encounters UNIKMO -> shares/repeats voluntarily -> new gift.
Primary metric: qualified visits -> purchase conversion, with separate reach, meaningful comments, share rate and recipient-driven referrals.
Start with A, B and D, using consistent channel/format where practical. Measure meaningful comment rate, shares and downstream traffic before scaling. Do not invent benchmarks. Decision: SCALE / ITERATE / STOP / NEEDS_MORE_EVIDENCE / WEBSITE_INTERVENTION_REQUIRED.
Before traffic push verify actual site copy, product pricing and checkout, creative rights, privacy/UGC consent, link targets and analytics tracking.
